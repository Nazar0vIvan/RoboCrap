# Prepare and animate planar probe scans on a placed workpiece

## Purpose / Big Picture

In **Measuring / Precise**, **Refine Frame** opens Properties so the operator can choose three CAD faces on a roughly placed workpiece, define a grid of probe contacts on each and save the definition as a **Refine Job**. Selecting that job and pressing **Generate Path** creates a linked **Scan Path**. Selecting the path and pressing **Dry Run** animates the existing on-screen robot. The first workpiece is the GE-22 end gauge. Calibration uses a separate fixed two-face measurement on that gauge.

This is a living ExecPlan. Maintain Progress, Surprises & Discoveries, Decision Log and Outcomes & Retrospective at each stopping point. The deliverables are the editor, path generation, disconnected playback and the common numerical input used by calibration sampling. Physical scanning means touching points using KUKA’s Robot Sensor Interface (RSI). Starting a new standalone controller procedure, fitting its returned observations and accepting a refined placement are subsequent work. A generated path alone never changes the active workpiece placement.

### 4a. Preview a rectangular grid on the active face

Use an orthonormal U/V basis measured in millimetres in {W}. Preserve the plan's deterministic convention: project the workpiece origin onto the plane for the origin; project and normalize the positive workpiece axis least parallel to the face normal for U (X, Y, Z order for ties); use the consistently oriented model normal crossed with U for V. Compute the bounded face extents in that basis, independently of camera and probe side. On each axis add the negative-side margin to the low bound and subtract the positive-side margin from the high bound. Each margin is finite and at least zero. Spacing is finite and positive, and count is an integer of at least two. The distance from first to last point is (count − 1) times spacing. Center that span in the remaining interval and step from the first point by exactly the entered spacing. Pair all U positions with all V positions. Index 1 is at the low end of each axis.

For a hypothetical face spanning 0–20 mm in U, with −U margin 2 mm, +U margin 4 mm, four points and 4 mm spacing, the usable interval is 2–16 mm.
